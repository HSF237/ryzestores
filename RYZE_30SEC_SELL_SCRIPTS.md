# RYZE — 30-Second Sell Scripts 🎬

"Do you need this? …Link in my bio." — one for every product. ~30 seconds each (~75 words spoken at a quick-but-natural pace).

**Use these AFTER launch** (Phase C), when your link is public and payments work.

**Delivery:** talk like you're telling a friend, energy up ~20%, don't read like an ad. Stay honest — real price, real shipping, never fake claims.

**Structure baked into each:** Hook → Problem → Product + 2 benefits → Price + free shipping → "Link in my bio."

---

## ⏱️ Timing & honesty
- ~75 words = ~30 seconds. Don't pad them.
- Read each price the way people say it ("twenty-two ninety-nine", not "two-two-nine-nine").
- 🚫 Never: fake discount, "best in the world", or calling the watch an Apple Watch.
- ✅ Always: real price, real 5–7 day shipping, honest benefit.
