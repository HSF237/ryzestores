# RYZE — Launch Roadmap 🚀

**Where you are now:** the store is built — real products, real images, honest copy, your logo, a working order system. The hard part is done. Now it's about **launching and getting your first sales.**

**Your reality:** ₹10,000 budget · selling in India (Cash sourcing via Meesho) · organic-first (free marketing) · founder is 14, so a parent/guardian holds the money + legal side.

**The golden rule:** don't wait for "perfect." Get to your **first 10 sales**, learn, then grow.

---

## Phase 0 — Setup (this week) 🔴 *must do before any real sale*

These are the grown-up + prep steps. Nothing sells until Phase 0 is done.

1. **Parent/guardian as account holder.** They own the bank account, Razorpay, and are the legal owner. You run it day-to-day.
2. **Bank account** (their name) — savings account with UPI/online banking.
3. **Razorpay account** (their name, linked to the bank) — so the store can take real online payments. *(I can guide you both through connecting it to the code when ready.)*
4. **Order 2–3 samples** of your hero products (Galaxy Projector, Sunset Lamp, Scalp Massager) — to check quality AND film your own content.
5. **Keep it honest/halal** — real prices, real delivery times (5–7 days), no fake reviews. Already done on the store. ✅

✅ *Done when:* a customer could pay you online and the money reaches your account.

---

## Phase 1 — Build an audience (Weeks 1–2) 🟢 *start NOW, in parallel*

You don't need payments working to start this. **Traffic is everything** — a great store with no visitors makes ₹0.

1. **Make social accounts** for RYZE: TikTok, Instagram (Reels), YouTube (Shorts). Same handle everywhere (e.g. @ryzestore).
2. **Pick ONE hero product** to push first (recommended: Galaxy Projector or Sunset Lamp — easiest to film, most eye-catching).
3. **Post 3–5 short videos a day.** 7–15 seconds, filmed on your phone, hook in the first 2 seconds. (Ask me for the video hook scripts — I'll write 10.)
4. **Don't sell hard yet** — just get views and followers. Reply to every "where can I buy?" comment.

✅ *Done when:* one of your videos crosses a few thousand views, or you hit ~100 followers.

---

## Phase 2 — First sales (Weeks 3–6) 💰

1. **Drive your audience to the store** — put the link in your bio and pin it in comments.
2. **When an order comes in:** your dashboard shows the customer's address + a "Order from Supplier" button → you place the Meesho order to their address → keep the profit.
3. **Reinvest every rupee of profit** into testing your next product.
4. **Collect real reviews** from happy buyers (your store only allows verified-buyer reviews — honest by design).

✅ *Milestones:* First sale → first 10 sales → first ₹10,000 month.

---

## Phase 3 — Grow (Month 2+) 📈

1. **Find your next 2–3 winners** using the daily product-research habit (TikTok #TikTokMadeMeBuyIt, Amazon Movers & Shakers, Google Trends).
2. **Start small paid ads** (₹300–₹500/day) — but ONLY on a product that already sells organically.
3. **Build a brand**, not just a store — pick RYZE's identity (e.g. "smart tech & cozy-room gadgets"), so people remember and return.
4. **Scale fulfillment** — when orders grow, switch worldwide products to CJ Dropshipping (auto-fulfillment).
