## 🎨 The style rule — read first

For a grid that looks like a real brand, **every image must share one style.** Paste this at the end of *every* prompt:

> **STYLE SUFFIX:** *dark charcoal near-black background (#0a0a0b), champagne gold accents (#c9a962), premium minimal e-commerce aesthetic, soft golden rim lighting, subtle glow, high contrast, clean negative space, modern bold sans-serif typography, luxury tech brand feel, 4k product photography, no clutter*

**Aspect ratio:** `4:5` (Instagram portrait) — in Midjourney add `--ar 4:5`

⚠️ **AI images are for *branded/atmosphere* posts only.** For actual **product** posts, use your **real product photos** — never an AI-generated fake of a product you sell. That would be dishonest (and customers notice).

# 💰 Honest offers you can actually run

Only promise what you can deliver — every one of these is safe:

| Offer | Why it works |
|---|---|
| **Free shipping on everything** | Already true. Biggest conversion lever in India. |
| **First 20 orders: ₹100 off** (code `RYZE100`) | Real scarcity — you genuinely stop at 20. **Honour it.** |
| **Bundle: any 2 room-glow items = ₹150 off** | Raises order value honestly |
| **Free gift with orders over ₹1,500** (e.g. stylus) | Cheap for you, feels generous |
| **Refer a friend: both get ₹100 off** | Free growth |

🚫 **Never do:** fake countdowns, "was ₹4,999" prices that were never real, "only 2 left" when you have unlimited. That's what everyone else does — being the honest one *is* your brand.
