## 📎 WHAT TO ATTACH (and when) — do this for every product
Veo can use images you attach as reference. Here's the rule for a 3-part ad:

| Part | What to ATTACH in Gemini | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Part 1** (hook/problem) | 🖼️ **Your real product image** | So the product in the video looks like the one you actually sell (honest + accurate) |
| **Part 2** (product in use) | 🖼️ **Your real product image** again | Keeps the product consistent across clips |
| **Final part** (ending) | 🖼️ **Your RYZE logo (`mylogo.png`)** | So the correct gold logo appears at the end — not an AI-drawn fake |

**For a 2-part ad:** attach the **product image** on Part 1, and attach **both the product image AND the logo** on Part 2 (the ending).

**Where to find your files:**
- Product images: `client/public/products/` (or use the clean photos you sourced)
- Logo: `client/public/mylogo.png`

**Tips when attaching:**
- Say in the prompt: *"use the attached product image as the exact product"* and *"use the attached logo unchanged."*
- If Veo redraws the product/logo weirdly, regenerate — never post a wrong-looking product or logo.
- If a part has no product on screen (e.g. a pure mood shot), you don't need to attach anything.

## Notes
- 🤝 **Honesty:** keep the product's real look/behaviour — don't exaggerate the glow beyond reality.
- 🧕 People are described modestly on purpose.
- 🎙️ Veo's voice is decent but may sound slightly robotic — you can instead **mute it and record your own voice** in CapCut (better for reach).
- ⏰ These are selling scripts ("link in bio") — use **after launch**; during tease phase swap for curiosity lines.
- 🔁 Keep the same room/lighting across a product's parts so the clips cut together seamlessly.
