**The golden rule before adding ANY of these: order ONE sample yourself first.** Check the real quality and delivery time before you sell it. Never sell something you haven't held.

## 1. Sunset Projection Lamp
A small lamp that projects a glowing sunset/rainbow onto walls. Huge on TikTok for "aesthetic room" and photo backdrops.

- **Source cost:** ~₹350–₹500

- **Where to source:** AliExpress (search "sunset projection lamp", pick 4.7★+ with 1,000+ orders) · CJ Dropshipping · Meesho (India/COD)
- **Content angle:** Turn off the lights → switch it on → film the room glowing. Pair with a trending audio. "POV: your room at golden hour, anytime."

## Best suppliers for worldwide (ranked for you)

1. **CJ Dropshipping** — *start here.* Free plan, 50+ global warehouses (cheaper worldwide shipping), connects to stores, can private-label later. Best all-rounder.
2. **AliExpress** — biggest catalog, great for sampling and testing. Only buy from sellers with **4.7★+ and high order counts**.
3. **Meesho / IndiaMART** — for the India (COD) portion of orders: local, cheap, 5–7 day delivery.

## How to vet a supplier (every time)
1. **Order a sample to yourself first** — non-negotiable.
2. Choose sellers with **4.7★+ ratings** and **lots of orders/reviews**.
3. Confirm they ship to your target countries, and get the **real** delivery time.
4. Ask their refund/defect policy — who pays for broken items?
5. Message them once — slow replies now = slow problem-solving later.

## How to add these to RYZE
