# RYZE — Voiceover Scripts 🎙️

Short spoken scripts for your product videos. Each is **8–15 seconds** — that's the sweet spot for Reels/TikTok/Shorts.

---

## 🎯 How to deliver them (read this once, it matters more than the words)

1. **Talk fast.** Slightly faster than normal conversation. Slow voiceovers get scrolled.
2. **First 2 seconds carry everything.** Start mid-thought — no "Hey guys, welcome back."
3. **Sound like a friend, not an ad.** Say it how you'd tell your cousin about a cool thing you bought.
4. **Record in a quiet room**, phone 15cm from your mouth, slightly to the side (stops popping).
5. **Energy up ~20%** from normal. It always sounds flatter on playback than it feels.
6. **Do 3 takes**, keep the best. Never the first one.
7. **Your own voice > TTS.** If you're shy, CapCut's free text-to-speech works — but real voice performs better.
8. **Never claim what isn't true.** No "best in the world", no fake discounts. Honest sells better anyway.

## 🎵 About music

- **TikTok / Instagram Reels:** add **trending audio inside the app** — free, licensed, and the algorithm favours it. Keep your voiceover on top, music low (~20% volume).
- **YouTube Shorts / paid ads:** use the original **`ryze_bg_music.mp3`** I generated — it's copyright-safe, no strikes.
- ⚠️ **Never** rip a popular song into your video file. Muted videos and copyright strikes aren't worth it.

---

## Mixing levels (in CapCut)
- Voiceover: **100%**
- Music: **15–25%**
- Always leave a beat of silence at the very end — it improves rewatch rate.

*Stay honest in every script. If the product doesn't do it, don't say it.* 🤲
