# RYZE — 10 Organic Video Hooks: Galaxy Star Projector

For TikTok, Instagram Reels & YouTube Shorts. Film vertical (9:16) on your phone. The **first 2 seconds decide everything** — show the projector ON, room glowing, immediately.

**Golden rules:**
- Hook in the first 2 seconds (visual + text on screen).
- Film in a dark room so the stars pop.
- Use a trending/calming audio (check what's trending that week).
- Keep videos 7–15 seconds.
- Post 3–5 a day. Most will flop; one will pop. That's normal.
- Stay honest — never claim it does something it doesn't.

---

### Hook 1 — The reveal
**On-screen text:** "POV: you turn your room into a galaxy 🌌"
**Shot:** Dark room → click remote → ceiling fills with stars. Slow pan up.
**Caption:** Turning my room into outer space ✨ #roomtransformation #galaxyprojector #roomdecor

### Hook 2 — Before / After
**On-screen text:** "My boring room vs. my room now"
**Shot:** 2 sec of plain room → swipe transition → same room glowing.
**Caption:** The upgrade was instant 🌌 #beforeandafter #roommakeover #aesthetic

### Hook 3 — Problem → solution
**On-screen text:** "Can't sleep? Try this instead of your phone."
**Shot:** Lying in bed, lights off, projector on ceiling, calm vibe.
**Caption:** My new bedtime routine 😴🌌 #sleeproutine #cozyroom #nightroutine

### Hook 4 — The price flex (honest)

### Hook 5 — Couple / friends angle

**On-screen text:** "Watch till the end 👀"
**Shot:** Slowly cycle through each colour mode. Satisfying, no talking.

**On-screen text:** "Would you put this in your room? 🌌"

**Caption:** Yes or no? 👇 #roomdecor #galaxyprojector #fyp

### Hook 10 — Trend-jack
**On-screen text:** Use the current trending phrase/sound + "...but in my room"
**Shot:** Match the beat drop to the moment the projector turns on.
**Caption:** Had to try the trend 🌌 #trending #roomtransformation #fyp

## After you post
- Reply to comments asking "where?" / "how much?" — that's buying intent. Pin a comment with your store link.
- Re-film the hooks that got the most views with small changes.
- Save your best-performing video and study *why* it worked, then repeat it.

## Honesty check (keep it halal)
- Only show what the projector really does.
- Don't fake the price or invent reviews.
- If shipping is 5–7 days, say so when people ask.
