# PART 1 — The Free Research System 🔍

Do this **30 minutes a day**. This is the skill that keeps making you money long after any tool or subscription ends.

## Source 1 — TikTok & Instagram Reels (best for spotting winners EARLY)
- Search hashtags: `#TikTokMadeMeBuyIt` · `#viralproduct` · `#amazonfinds` · `#gadgets` · `#roomdecor`
- **Filter to "last 7 days"** — you want what's rising NOW, not last year's winner.
- 🔑 **Read the comments.** The real signal isn't views — it's people commenting *"where can I buy this?"*, *"link?"*, *"need this"*. That's proven buying intent.
- If a video has 500k+ views and buying-intent comments → strong candidate.

## Source 2 — YouTube (best for validation + learning)
- Search: `"winning dropshipping products [current month] [year]"` — many channels post monthly picks.
- ⚠️ **Don't just copy their picks blindly** — thousands of people watch those videos, so those products get saturated fast. Use them to spot *categories* and *trends*, then find your own angle or a similar product.
- Also search the product name + "review" to check real quality before you sell it.

## Source 3 — Google Trends (best for checking if it's rising or dying)
- Go to trends.google.com → search the product → set region to **India** → last 12 months.
- ✅ Line going **up** = get in.
- ❌ Line going **down** = skip it, you're late.
- ➡️ Flat but steady = evergreen, safe but competitive.

## Source 4 — Amazon Movers & Shakers (best for real sales data)
- Amazon's free "Movers & Shakers" page shows products whose sales are **spiking right now**.
- If something's climbing on Amazon, it often goes viral on TikTok shortly after. Great early signal.

## Source 5 — Meesho itself (your actual supplier)
- Search your product idea on Meesho → sort by popularity.
- Check: **4.0★+ rating**, **high review count** (500+ is great), reasonable price.
- If nobody on Meesho sells it well, you can't source it reliably — skip.

## The winning-product checklist ✅
Score every idea. A real winner hits most of these:

1. **2-second rule** — can someone understand it instantly, with no sound? (Transformations, satisfying motion, obvious "wow.")
2. **Solves a problem** or triggers a feeling (comfort, fun, self-care, "I need that").
3. **Not in every local shop** — hard to find offline = they buy online.
4. **Lightweight & sturdy** — cheap to ship, doesn't arrive broken.
5. **Sells ₹400–₹2,500** — impulse-buy range.
6. **3x markup possible** — buy at X, sell at 3X (covers product, shipping, refunds, profit).
7. **Rising, not dying** (Google Trends).
8. **Sourceable** — good Meesho listing with real reviews.

❌ **Fails rule 1 or 6 → skip it**, no matter how cool it seems.

# PART 3 — Red Flags 🚩 (what to avoid)

- **Clothing/shoes with sizes** — high returns, sizing complaints. (Meesho's top category is ethnic wear, but it's brutal for beginners.)
- **Fragile items** (glass, ceramics) — arrive broken, eat your profit.
- **Heavy/bulky items** — shipping kills the margin.
- **Electronics with warranty claims** (big appliances) — support nightmare.
- **Anything you can't film in 2 seconds** — you can't market it organically.
- **Copies/fakes of branded goods** — dishonest, and illegal. Never.
- **Anything haram** — alcohol, gambling, etc. Keep RYZE clean. ✅

# PART 4 — Your Weekly Routine 📅

- **Daily (30 min):** scroll TikTok/Reels with the hashtags, note anything with buying-intent comments.
- **Weekly:** take your top 3 ideas → check Google Trends (India) → check Meesho for a good supplier → score with the checklist.
- **Monthly:** add 1–2 new winners; drop products that got zero interest after real content effort.

**The truth about winners:** you don't "find" one perfect product — you **test many**. Most flop. One pops. That's normal and it's how everyone does it. Keep testing, stay honest, keep posting. 🚀
