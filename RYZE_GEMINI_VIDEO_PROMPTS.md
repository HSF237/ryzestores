# RYZE — Gemini (Veo) Multi-Part Video Ad Prompts 🎬

Gemini makes 10s clips, so each ad = **2–3 parts** you stitch together in CapCut into one 20–30s Reel.

## How to use
1. Generate **each part separately** in Gemini (paste the prompt).
2. Put them in order in **CapCut** → export as one video.
3. Add **trending audio** in the Instagram/TikTok app + your voiceover (scripts are in `RYZE_30SEC_SELL_SCRIPTS.md`).
4. Only the **last part** shows the RYZE logo — don't repeat it every clip.

## Two rules for EVERY prompt
- Add at the end: **"vertical 9:16 format, cinematic, dark background with gold accents, 4k"** (this keeps size + brand consistent).
- Keep the product **realistic** — don't ask for effects way beyond what the real item does. Honesty = your brand.

## Continuity trick
Each part is a separate generation, so keep the **same room, same lighting, same colours** described in all parts of one ad, so the cuts feel connected.

## ⚡ Final tips
- **Always end on the logo** — one clean brand moment per video.
- **Keep it honest** — realistic product behaviour, real glow, no fake magic.
- **Text + price + "link in bio"** → add in CapCut, not in the Gemini video (AI misspells).
- **Trending audio** in-app > baked-in music for reach.
